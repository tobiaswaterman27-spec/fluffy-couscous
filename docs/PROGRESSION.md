# AFTER HOURS — progression, the eight ways out, and how the night gets harder

The player starts with nothing in the public mall. Every key or card opens rooms that hold the next key or card.
No room ever holds its own key, and the internal doors between back-of-house rooms are sealed, so locked rooms
can't be bypassed (except with the fire axe, below). **After Hours > Check Progression** in Unity checks all of this
automatically, including the shuffle groups in the built mall.

## Items move every run

Important items are **shuffled at the start of every run**, and again every time you're caught. They only move
between rooms that need the **same key or card**. So a key from Mall management might be in the Records office or
the Roof access plant room instead. Progression can therefore never break, but you can't memorise where things are.
Each room has six clear spots by its inner door, and every open shop counter or stock room is a spot for the
public items. Saves keep the run's layout. In co-op the host deals the items out and everyone sees the same thing.

| Lock tier (shuffle group) | Rooms | Items dealt out over them |
|---|---|---|
| open | every open shop (counter or stock room) | staff access card, screwdriver, utility knife, circuit module, storage key, battery |
| card_staff | Staff room & lockers, Maintenance workshop, Staff canteen | locker key, maintenance card, pliers, spanner, replacement switch, maintenance key |
| card_maintenance | Electrical room, Maintenance L1, Switch room | **one** fuse, power connector, plant room key, wire cutters, restricted area card |
| key_maintenance | car park plant room, car park maintenance store | **one** fuse, service tunnel key, adjustable wrench, jerry can (petrol) |
| key_plantroom | Plant room L1, Air handling plant, Water tanks | mechanical handle, **the only gear**, valve wheel |
| card_restricted | Mall management, Records office, Roof access plant, Staff exit lobby | security key, security token, lift key, fire exit key, shop master key, combination dial, roof hatch key, special access card |
| key_security | Security office | security card, loading bay key, loading bay remote, control switch |
| key_loading | Loading bay, Goods in | pry bar, door actuator, machine part |
| axe_cabinet | fire axe cabinets in up to four stairwells | the fire axe (only one cabinet has it) |

The main entrance key always stays in the locked staff locker.

## The standby generator

The car park plant room (maintenance key) holds the **standby generator**. At night the door release, the car park
exit shutter motor, the dock controller and the staff exit mechanism all run on the emergency circuit it feeds.
Routes 1, 2, 3 and 7 need it running. An amber "EMERGENCY CIRCUIT" lamp by each of those panels lights up when
it's on.

To start it:
1. Pour in the **jerry can** (hold to pour).
2. Fit a **fuse** in GEN CONTROL.
3. Pull the cord. The first pulls only cough, and every pull is loud.

Once it's running it's very loud and thuds through the building, so Elliot hears it from far away. That fades as
it settles. You can switch it off again, and anything it already released stays open.

There are **only two fuses**, and the car park exit needs both: one for the generator and one for the shutter supply.

## The fire axe (the other way through)

A red "FIRE AXE - BREAK GLASS" cabinet hangs beside the fire point in a few stairwells; only one holds the axe.
Breaking the glass is loud.

The axe can:
- Chop through any locked **wooden** door (staff, restricted and storage rooms). It takes six very loud blows.
- Force the rooftop cage, in place of the pry bar.

It **splits after three jobs**. Steel doors (plant, electrical, security, maintenance) can't be chopped. The routes
are all solvable without the axe; it's a shortcut that costs noise and choices.

## The eight ways out

| # | Route | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main entrance | security card swiped + **security token** in its slot + door release switch (Security office), main entrance key in the door lock, **generator running** |
| 2 | Car park exit | shutter supply fuse (plant room), control switch in the ramp panel, **unbolt the gearbox cover (spanner)** and fit **the gear**, motor switch, **generator running** |
| 3 | Loading bay | circuit module + **power connector** in the dock controller, loading bay remote, **generator running** |
| 4 | Emergency exit | the chain: **cut it (wire cutters) or unlock its padlock (fire exit key)**. Then **push the bar**: alarm bell and beacon for **15 seconds** before the magnetic lock lets go, or first **cut the maglock cable (utility knife)** and it opens silently. |
| 5 | Rooftop | roof hatch key, force the cage (**pry bar or fire axe**), **undo the rusted hatch bolts (spanner)**, climb down |
| 6 | Service tunnel | maintenance key (gate), **work the seized stem loose (spanner)**, fit and turn the valve wheel (long, loud) |
| 7 | Restricted staff exit | special access card, door actuator in its housing, **replacement switch in the control box**, **generator running** |
| 8 | Hidden | the door behind cinema screen 3, pull back the crate stack, unscrew the floor hatch (screwdriver), fit **the gear** and the mechanical handle to the latch and turn it |

**Choices:**
- There is only one gear, so you can take the car park exit **or** the hidden hatch, not both.
- There are only two fuses.
- The axe saves a long key chain but only lasts three jobs.
- At the fire door you can wait out the alarm or find the cable first.
- Starting the generator brings Elliot to the car park, which can also be used to pull him away from you.

## The night gets worse (NightDirector)

The clock starts at 11:30 PM; one real minute is two and a half minutes of night (it's on the pause screen). Elliot's
**threat level** follows whichever of these is further along: the time, or how close anyone is to getting out.

| Threat | When | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | start | keeps to the car park unless something draws him out |
| 1 | 15 min, or a third of any route done, or 3 steps done anywhere | roams the whole building; hears and sees a bit better |
| 2 | 40 min, or 60% of a route done, or 7 steps done | drifts towards places where someone has been working on a way out; quicker when searching |
| 3 | 70 min, or **one step from escape** on any route | moves with purpose, listens hard, keeps checking those places |

Each rise is marked by a scream somewhere in the building. His sprint barely changes (you can still just outrun him),
but his searching and stalking get much faster and his hearing reaches much further. Being caught in single-player
starts the run over: the threat resets, and the items move again.

## Endings

Every way out has its own last line in the credits. They also show your time inside and how many times you were
caught.
