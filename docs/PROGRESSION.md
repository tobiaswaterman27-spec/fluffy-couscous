# AFTER HOURS — progression and the eight escape routes

The player starts with nothing in the public mall. Every key or card opens rooms that contain the next key or card.
No room ever contains its own key, and the internal doors between back-of-house rooms are sealed so locked rooms
can't be bypassed. **After Hours > Check Progression** in Unity verifies all of this automatically.

## Who opens what

| Key / card | Found in | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| — (start) | public areas, open shops, food court, toilets, stairwells, car park, service corridors (via toilet passages / open shop back doors) | |
| Staff access card | an open shop's counter | staff passages, Staff room & lockers, Maintenance workshop, Staff canteen |
| Locker key | Staff room | the one locked staff locker |
| Maintenance card | Maintenance workshop | Electrical room, Maintenance L1, Switch room |
| Maintenance key | Staff canteen | car park plant room & maintenance store, service tunnel gate |
| Plant room key | Maintenance L1 | Plant room L1, Air handling plant, Water tanks |
| Restricted area card | Maintenance L1 | Mall management, Records, Staff exit lobby, Roof access plant |
| Security key | Mall management | Security office |
| Loading bay key | Security office | Loading bay, Goods in |
| Shop master key | Mall management | locked shop shutters and shop back doors |
| Storage key | food court kiosk / shops | storage rooms |

## The eight routes (all roughly equal effort; several share early steps)

| # | Route | Clue in the world | Requirements | Final sequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main entrance | dark glass doors with a key lock; "MAIN DOORS" release panel in Security | security card swiped + door release switch on (Security office) + main entrance key (locked staff locker) in the door lock | the automatic doors grind open (very loud) |
| 2 | Car park exit | exit shutter at the top of the ramp; empty fuse holder "SHUTTER SUPPLY"; control cabinet missing a switch | fuse in the plant-room fuse box + control switch fitted + motor switch on | the shutter crawls up, slowly and loudly |
| 3 | Loading bay | dock controller with an empty module slot and a remote receiver | circuit module fitted + loading bay remote used | a dock shutter rises |
| 4 | Emergency exit | fire exit chained and padlocked | cut the chain (wire cutters) + unlock the door (fire exit key) | push the heavy door open |
| 5 | Rooftop | roof door; fire-escape ladder hatch inside a padlocked cage | roof hatch key + force the cage (pry bar) | climb down the ladder |
| 6 | Service tunnel | 80 m tunnel; pressure door with a bare valve stem | maintenance key (gate) + valve wheel fitted and turned (6 s, loud) | the pressure door unlocks |
| 7 | Restricted staff exit | card reader + empty mechanism housing above the exit | restricted card (lobby) + special access card + door actuator fitted | the door mechanism engages |
| 8 | Hidden | unmarked stiff door behind cinema screen 3 | find the door, pull the heavy crate stack back, unscrew the floor hatch (screwdriver), fit the handle to the latch and turn it | drop through the hatch |

Every route makes noise at the end. Part 13 adds the daylight ending; Part 17 adds the co-op rule that all
surviving players must get out within five seconds of each other.
